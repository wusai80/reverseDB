Five-project fresh evaluation and module analysis — 2026-11-10

All five principal existing development projects were run sequentially with
fixed original protocols, full data and unchanged semantic engines/policy.
No performance-driven reruns or extra samples. Old batches remain separate.

These are ratios of arm medians within distinct timing boundaries, not pooled

execution/measurement variation and cannot be attributed to optimization.

Framework outcomes: Java fusion; Fossil deletion; original for alf.io, Oscar,
and beets. A generic source-description adapter replays the existing beets
admission checks. No project-specific rule was added to the framework core.
Experimental gains in Oscar/beets do not override unresolved semantic/backend
obligations. Oscar's fresh paired interval is [1.0358,1.0977]; its earlier batch
had [0.9063,1.0844]. Both are retained; do not call it an intrinsic failure or
claim an automatically admitted speedup. Beets fresh paired interval is
[1.0899,1.1217], still under its explicit successful-read deployment contract.

Java deletion alone removes one query but is 3.02% slower in mixed median here.
Fusion+deletion has a 2.53% lower median than fusion alone, with 5/8 wins. Its
geometric incremental ratio 1.017 has lower bound 0.979, below the unchanged
1.02 criterion. The selector therefore keeps fusion alone.
